Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is an evolving field that can improve efficiency and productivity across government. This section highlights key policies, standards, and guidance to ensure its secure and responsible use.

Using AI securely

As set out in the AI Playbook for the UK government, all government AI technologies and services must be secure and resilient. They should comply with the Secure by Design principles and the government’s Cyber Security Standard.

Government AI Security Team

The Government AI Security Team is part of the Government Cyber Unit (GCU) and provides a holistic view of security risks and threats related to the use of Artificial Intelligence. It is a team providing governance, advice and consultancy services as well as investigating technical solutions for the UK government.

Training and events

As part of the Government’s acceleration of AI adoption, we are looking at ways to support upskilling civil servants to help lay the foundations for securing AI across government. We will aim to link to key strategic partners’ available training as well as investigate a variety of methods for bespoke training, including virtual, bite size, self learning and in-person events.

See the training and events page (sign in required) for more details and past sessions.

Community

A generative artificial intelligence (AI) security community exists to share insights and practical guidance into the security risks of AI and generative AI in particular.

View community details on GOV.UK

Resources

There are a range of resources that provide valuable insights and guidance to AI and its security practices. To gain a comprehensive understanding of AI and its security implications, read:

  • Secure by Design
  • GOV.UK – Code of Practice for the Cyber Security of AI
  • GOV.UK – Artificial Intelligence Playbook for the UK Government
  • GOV.UK – A pro-innovation approach to AI regulation
  • GDS – The Incubator for Artificial Intelligence
  • NCSC – Guidelines for secure AI system development

Contact

If you have any questions about AI security, email the team at ai.security@dsit.gov.uk.

This team sits with the Government Cyber Unit, which is part of the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology.
